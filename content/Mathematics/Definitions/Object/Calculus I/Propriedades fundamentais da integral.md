Sejam $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ tais que $a<b$, sejam $\ell \in \mathbb{N}_0, \lambda>0, c \in \mathbb{R}$, e sejam $\gamma_1, \gamma_2 \in \mathbb{R}$. Sejam $f, g: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ polinômios.
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \bullet \int_a^b x^{\ell} d x=\frac{b^{\ell+1}-a^{\ell+1}}{\ell+1} \\ \\

& \bullet \int_a^b\left(\gamma_1 f(x)+\gamma_2 g(x)\right) d x=\gamma_1 \int_a^b f(x) d x+\gamma_2 \int_a^b g d x \\ \\

& \bullet \int_a^b f(x+c) d x=\int_{a+c}^{b+c} f(x) d x \\ \\

& \bullet \int_a^b f(\lambda x) d x=\frac{1}{\lambda} \int_{\lambda a}^{\lambda b} f(x) d x \\ \\

& \bullet \int_a^b f(x) d x=-\int_{b}^{a} f(x) d x
\end{aligned}
$$
As $4$ primeiras são da lista 2.
